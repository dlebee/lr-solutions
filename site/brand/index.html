Brand kit

How L&R Solutions looks and sounds. Use it for proposals, decks, signage, social posts and anything with our name on it.

The basics

Name
L&R Solutions. Always a capital L, an ampersand and a capital R with no spaces, then "Solutions". Not "LR", "L and R" or "L & R". For web addresses and email: lr-solutions.com.
Tagline
Your kitchen, connected.
What we do
Sales systems, central kitchen technology and custom software for food businesses.
In one line
We design, integrate and support the systems behind your sales and your central kitchen, and build custom software when off-the-shelf won't do.

Logo

A serif L&R set in DM Serif Display, with the ampersand in LR Blue to show the partnership at the heart of the company. Underneath, SOLUTIONS is set in spaced-out capitals in Plus Jakarta Sans. The serif brings a hospitality feel and the clean sans brings the technology. All files are outlined vector shapes, so they look identical everywhere, even without the fonts installed.

Colour

Blue and white. White carries the page, LR Blue carries the brand, and Navy is for text. Keep it to roughly 70% white, 20% blue, 10% navy.

Typography

Two free Google Fonts. DM Serif Display for the logo and big headlines brings the hospitality feel. Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else (subheadings, body, buttons, labels) brings the clean tech feel. Never set body text in the serif.

Do & don't

Do

  • Use lots of white space
  • Use LR Blue for the one main action on a page
  • Use the stacked logo where there's room, the horizontal one in tight spaces
  • Show real systems in use: POS screens, kitchen displays, production lines

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the logo, or retype it in another font
  • Add gradients, shadows or outlines to the logo
  • Mix in other bright colours
  • Use generic stock "tech" imagery
