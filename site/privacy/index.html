Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Version française

L&R Solutions is a Quebec company. This policy explains what personal information we collect through lr-solutions.com, why, and how you can exercise your rights under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25).

Person in charge of personal information

Our person in charge of the protection of personal information is our President. To reach them, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We reply within 30 days.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Cookies and consent

Analytics are off by default. Google Analytics is only loaded if you click “Accept” in the cookie notice. It then sets the _ga and _ga_* cookies, which expire after 13 months. Google signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Your choice is stored in your browser for 12 months. You can change it at any time with Cookie preferences; if you decline, we remove the analytics cookies.

Service providers and information outside Quebec

We use the following providers. Some of them store or process information outside Quebec, including in the United States. Before using them, we assessed that their contractual and security measures give your information adequate protection.

  • Cloudflare: human check and delivery of contact-form emails
  • Microsoft 365: our business email, where enquiries are received
  • GitHub (Microsoft): website hosting
  • Google: Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads its fonts

Your rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format, withdraw your consent, or ask that it be deleted or no longer disseminated. Send your request through the contact form (“Privacy request”). We may ask you to confirm your identity. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Security

Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need it to answer you. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the people concerned, as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the date of the last update at the top.
